Why they're here.

What this site is for, and what it is not.

Good People has one habit: every visit introduces someone who made the world a little kinder. No feed, no ranking, no score. One person, a photograph, a few sentences, and a reason to feel hopeful.

Nobody is here for being famous, wealthy, or powerful. Some of the people on this site happen to be well known, but that is not why they are here. They are here for what they gave to others: patience with a child, a door opened to books, a love of the natural world passed on, encouragement for someone who had stopped believing in themselves. The question asked of every entry is simple. Did this person leave others better than they found them?

There are things this site will never have. No likes, no leaderboards, no politics, no arguing about who deserves it more. Everyone here is human, with everything that comes with that. The site celebrates the good they did, not a myth.

Every photograph comes from Wikimedia Commons and carries its photographer and licence beneath it. The short reflections are this site's own words, written for it. They are not quotations, and they should never be attributed to the people pictured.

Know someone who belongs here? The site is open source. Suggest a person on GitHub with a verified description and a freely licensed photograph.
